FIVE DAYS.
NO SHORTCUTS.

Salmon, salt and smoke. And all the time it takes.

01/ 05From fish to finished craft
01

The fish

Salmon from Icelandic land-based farms is selected and hand-filleted. Care begins at the workbench.

02

The salt

The smoke master dry-salts each fillet with care for an even cure. The salmon rests in salt for a day.

03

Rinsing and drying

The salt is rinsed away. The fillets dry in a gentle airflow for another day.

04

The smoke

The salmon is cold-smoked over beechwood or traditional dried sheep dung for a day. The kilns have been in use since 1986.

05

The finishing

Cooling and further drying firm the fillet. From start to finish, the process takes five days.
